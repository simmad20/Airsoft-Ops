## English (en-US)

**App name** (28)
```
Airsoft Ops: Game Mode Timer
```

**Short description** (80)
```
Spike, VIP, Battle Royale & more: timer, announcer and synced Bluetooth speakers
```

**Full description**
```
Airsoft Ops turns your phone into the game master for airsoft, paintball, laser tag and Nerf.

SEVEN GAME MODES
• Spike: attackers plant at site A or B, defenders defuse before the timer runs out. Half defuse, freely adjustable times.
• VIP Escort: get the VIP to the extraction point before the hunters take them out.
• Search & Destroy: destroy or hold objectives, no respawn, sides swap every round.
• Battle Royale: several teams, a shrinking zone and supply drops if you want them.
• Hostage Rescue: free the hostage and bring them to the extraction point.
• Infection: whoever gets hit switches sides. Survive until the end or infect everyone.
• Murder Mystery: secret roles such as murderer, medic, detective, twins and more. Players with a phone see their role on their own device, everyone else looks at it in secret one after another, with a lock against cheating.

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
• Huge hold areas to plant and defuse, even with gloves and under pressure.
• Announcer in English and German (two voices each) with countdown, beeps and a radio effect.
• The screen stays on during a round.
• Works offline. Only sessions with several phones need internet.

SEVERAL PHONES, ONE ROUND
Connect several phones by code or QR code: every Bluetooth speaker on the field beeps in sync, spectators follow the game live, and in Murder Mystery everyone gets their secret role on their own phone. Players without the app simply join in the browser.

TWO DESIGNS
Classic, in the look of a classic bomb timer, or Modern with an animated card deck and artwork for every mode.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
Free, no ads, no account, no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone.
```
